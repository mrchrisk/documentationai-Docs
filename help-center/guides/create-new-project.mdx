---
title: Creating a New Project Space
description: Guide to creating a new space in BosOn for your project.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Spaces menu">
    Click on "Spaces" in the left sidebar to view all available spaces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create new space">
    Click the "+ New Space" button at the top-right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in details">
    Enter the project name and description.
    Optional: Add members and set permissions here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click "Create". Your new project space is ready!
  </Step>
</Steps>
