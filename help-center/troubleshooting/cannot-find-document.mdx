---
title: Cannot Find My Document
description: Quick fix if a document seems missing in BosOn.
---

## Symptoms

- Document does not appear in search
- Folder appears empty

## Causes

- Wrong space or folder
- Filter or tag applied

## Solution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear filters and tags">
    Check for any active filters or tags in your current view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch spaces">
    Switch to the correct space from the sidebar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try global search">
    Use the top search bar with part of the document name.
  </Step>
</Steps>
